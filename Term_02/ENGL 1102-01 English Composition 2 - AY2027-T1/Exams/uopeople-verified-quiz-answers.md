**1. Imagine you are tasked with writing an essay on 'Environmental Conservation.' To make your topic more specific, you decide to focus on plastic pollution in oceans. Which of the following approaches best illustrates the process of narrowing the focus of a topic?**
- Planning your essay based on the general importance of environmental issues without specifying a particular focus.
- Dividing Environmental Conservation into smaller areas like deforestation, air pollution, and plastic pollution, then focusing on one subtopic.
- Copying points from a well-known article on Environmental Conservation to use in your essay.
- Writing a detailed outline of the essay structure, including introduction, body, and conclusion.

**Answer: Dividing Environmental Conservation into smaller areas like deforestation, air pollution, and plastic pollution, then focusing on one subtopic.** — CONFIRMED CORRECT
*Source: Readings, Hall & Wallace, Ch. 4.1 — The Writing Process: Prewriting and Drafting.*

**2. Match the goal of science with its description.**

Goals: 1. Describe 2. Predict 3. Explain 4. Control
Descriptions: a. Determining the causes of behavior by identifying relationships between variables. b. Making careful observations to understand phenomena. c. Using information to forecast when an event or behavior will occur. d. Applying knowledge to bring about changes in behavior or phenomena.

- 1-b, 2-c, 3-d, 4-a
- 1-c, 2-d, 3-a, 4-b
- 1-b, 2-c, 3-a, 4-d
- 1-b, 2-d, 3-a, 4-c

**Answer: 1-b, 2-c, 3-a, 4-d** — CONFIRMED CORRECT
*Source: Readings, Serdikoff, Ch. 1, Section 3 — Goals of Science.*

**4. Mathias Mehl and his colleagues tested the claim about gender differences in word usage using the method of ___________**
- Intuition.
- Empiricism.
- Authority.
- Rationalism.

**Answer: Empiricism.** — CONFIRMED CORRECT
*Source: Web-confirmed (Mehl et al.'s word-count study — women ~16,215 words/day vs. men's ~15,669 — is the standard textbook example of empiricism: testing a claim via direct observation/measurement rather than assumption or authority).*

**6. Which of the following is an example of a functional relationship in scientific research?**
- The description of behavior without explaining its causes.
- The prediction of traffic accidents based on weather patterns.
- Correlation between medical marijuana use and pain relief.
- The relationship between temperature and the rate of reaction.

**Answer: Correlation between medical marijuana use and pain relief.** — CONFIRMED CORRECT
**"The relationship between temperature and the rate of reaction" — CONFIRMED WRONG.**
*Note: this course's quiz key treats "functional relationship" more loosely than the strict experimental-manipulation definition — it's scored here as an observed relationship between two variables (marijuana use and pain relief), closer to the general "relationship between variables" framing used in the Goals of Science material. Do not default to the stricter textbook definition (independent variable manipulation → dependent variable change) for this specific course's quiz.*
